     appear frequently in the FCA column, those corrections are on-target.
     If CTSpawn dominates, the z-filter may be confusing large-tile size
     for z-overlap; inspect those plots specifically.
  2. Open vs_fca/ plots for each z_tol — check that the KDTree-assigned tile
     (teal) sits geographically closer to the player than the FCA tile (red).
     A teal tile on the opposite side of the radar from the player = overcorrection.
  3. Open adjacent_pairs/ plots — these show the sensitivity boundary. The
     pair where corrections start to look geographically wrong sets your
     upper bound; the pair where they all still look correct sets the floor.
  4. Pick the z_tol that maximises correct corrections (Catwalk/Underpass)
     while minimising incorrect ones (large-tile confusion in flat areas).
     Update Z_TOL in ztol_sweep.py and re-run for confirmation.
